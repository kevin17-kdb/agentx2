# Central Library Services & Rules

## Section 1: Timings
The Central Library is open 08:00 AM - 08:00 PM on working days and 09:00 AM - 05:00 PM on Saturdays. It remains closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Section 2: Borrowing Limits
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for 15 days. Reference books, journals, and theses may not be borrowed and are for in-library use only.

## Section 3: Fines
A late fine of Rs. 5 per day per book applies. Books lost must be replaced or the replacement cost paid within 15 days.

## Section 4: Digital Access
The library subscribes to IEEE, ACM, and Springer digital libraries. Access is available on-campus via the intranet and off-campus through the remote access portal using institutional credentials.

## Section 5: Printer & Photocopy Services
Printing and photocopy facilities are available at the ground floor. Students load credit via the fee portal at Rs. 1 per black-and-white page.
