# Transport & Campus Commute Services

## Section 1: Bus Routes
Four campus bus routes operate daily: Route 12 Mehdipatnam (pickup 06:40 AM, drop 04:30 PM), Route 07 Kukatpally (pickup 07:00 AM, drop 05:00 PM), Route 21 Uppal (pickup 06:50 AM, drop 04:45 PM), and Route 05 Dilsukhnagar (pickup 06:30 AM, drop 04:15 PM).

## Section 2: Semester Pass
Transport passes are issued at the transport office between the 1st and 10th of each month. Monthly pass: Rs. 1,200; Semester pass: Rs. 6,000.

## Section 3: Campus Bicycles
Shared bicycles are available near the main gate and hostel entrances. Reserve via the campus app; free for the first 30 minutes.

## Section 4: Visitors & Parking
Visitor parking is available at the main gate. Two-wheelers registered with the security office may park at designated lots behind each academic block.
