# Hostel Regulations & Student Services Handbook

## Section 1: Curfew & Gate Passes
In-time for campus hostels is 08:30 PM for all residents. Night out passes must be submitted 24 hours in advance via the Student Portal and approved by the Warden.

## Section 2: Mess Timings
Breakfast 07:00 - 09:00 AM, Lunch 12:30 - 02:00 PM, Dinner 07:30 - 09:30 PM. Special dietary requests must be registered with the mess secretary by Sunday evening.

## Section 3: Wi-Fi & Internet
Campus Wi-Fi is available on the SREC-ResNet SSID. Credentials are provisioned via the intranet portal. Guests may register for a 48-hour visitor pass at the IT Helpdesk.

## Section 4: Laundry Services
Laundry collection happens every Saturday at the ground floor office of each hostel block.

## Section 5: Grievances & Complaints
Infrastructure, mess quality, or Wi-Fi complaints must be logged via the Student Services desk or emailed to student-services@srec.ac.in. Average resolution SLA is 48 hours.

## Section 6: Warden Contact
Reach the warden at warden@srec.ac.in or extension 204 between 9 AM and 6 PM.
