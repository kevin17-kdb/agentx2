# Scholarship Criteria & Financial Aid Guidelines

## Section 1: Management Merit Scholarship
Awarded Rs. 25,000 per annum to the top 3 rankers of each branch based on semester-end CGPA. Applications close September 30. Marksheet copies are mandatory.

## Section 2: State Post-Matric Scholarship
Available to students with family income below Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum and 60% or higher marks in the previous examination. Provides full fee waiver plus Rs. 15,000 stipend. Applications close August 20.

## Section 3: Women in STEM Fellowship
Rs. 40,000 per annum for female students with CGPA 8.0 or above enrolled in CSE, ECE, or IT branches. Applications close September 5. A statement of purpose is required.

## Section 4: Disbursement Schedule
Approved scholarships are disbursed in two equal installments: October and February. Directly credited to the registered bank account linked to the student portal.

## Section 5: Renewal
Scholarships are renewable yearly provided the student maintains a CGPA of 7.5 or above and has no active backlogs.
