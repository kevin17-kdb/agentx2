# Grievance Redressal & Service Level Agreements

## Section 1: Channels
Students may file grievances through the Student Services Agent, the student portal, or email to student-services@vasavi.edu.in.

## Section 2: Resolution SLA
- Infrastructure complaints: 48 hours
- Mess quality complaints: 72 hours
- Wi-Fi / network issues: 24 hours
- Ragging or harassment (confidential): 12 hours with direct escalation to the Anti-Ragging Committee

## Section 3: Escalation
If a grievance is not resolved within the SLA, it automatically escalates to the Dean of Student Affairs, then to the Principal.

## Section 4: Anonymity
Grievances may be filed anonymously. Anonymous complaints are investigated without revealing the complainant's identity unless legally required.
