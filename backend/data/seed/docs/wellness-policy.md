# Student Wellness & Counseling Policy

## Section 1: On-Campus Counseling
Free and confidential counseling is available Monday to Saturday, 10 AM - 5 PM, at the Student Wellness Center (Medical Room Annex). Students may self-refer or be referred by faculty.

## Section 2: Wellness Resources
- 24x7 support helpline: +91 90000 12345
- Peer support groups meet every Friday at the Wellness Center.
- Stress management workshops are held monthly in the Central Library AV Room.

## Section 3: Academic Overload Policy
Students facing academic overload (defined as 2 or more major assessments within the same week combined with other commitments) are encouraged to speak with their mentor for workload redistribution and deadline extension requests.

## Section 4: Leave for Mental Health
One mental-health day per semester is granted to every student without impacting attendance below the condonation threshold, subject to mentor notification.

## Section 5: Recommended Practices
Students reporting elevated stress are advised to take 15-minute restorative breaks every 90 minutes, maintain a 7-hour sleep schedule during exam weeks, and use the fitness complex for low-intensity activity.
