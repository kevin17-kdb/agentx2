# Academic & Examination Regulations 2025-2026

## Section 1: Evaluation Structure
The evaluation structure is 40% Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) + 60% Semester End Examination (SEE). Minimum pass mark in CIE is 40% and overall 45%.

## Section 2: Semester Examinations
End-semester examinations are conducted in November and May each academic year. A student with pending dues, library fines, or disciplinary action may be withheld from appearing.

## Section 3: Makeup Examination Policy
Makeup examinations are granted strictly for students who missed regular mid-term or end-semester examinations due to officially sanctioned medical emergencies, approved sports representation, or placement drive collisions. Approval must be obtained from the Dean of Examinations and Head of Department within 3 days of the missed exam.

## Section 4: Backlog Clearance
A student must clear all active backlogs before the commencement of the eighth semester to be eligible for provisional degree conferral. Backlog examinations are conducted twice a year.

## Section 5: Elective Registration
Elective registration opens in the first two weeks of each semester. Students must have completed prerequisite courses with a minimum grade of 'C' before enrolling in advanced electives.
